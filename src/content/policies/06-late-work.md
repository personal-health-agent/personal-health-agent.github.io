---
title: "Late work"
order: 6
---

Each student gets three "grace days" across the term for take-home work, no questions asked. Beyond those days, take-home work is graded down 10% per day unless arranged in advance. Grace days and late submissions do not apply to scheduled in-class checkpoints: the two quizzes, the Week 9 proposal presentation, or the Week 14 final demo/showcase.
