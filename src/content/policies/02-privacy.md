**BINF 4070 Personal Health Data Lab** is the course's optional OAuth application. Some approved labs may use it so eligible students can authorize read-only access to their own Google Health activity, health metrics, sleep, and other disclosed data without sharing their Google password. Course notebooks retrieve the authorized data into each student's own Colab session; the helper does not retain OAuth credentials or health records, and course staff do not receive them. Live data is optional: synthetic data supports the same required work, and students can decline or revoke access at any time without penalty.
