**BINF 4070 Personal Health Data Lab** is the course's optional OAuth application. Some approved labs may use it so eligible students can authorize read-only access to their own Google Health activity and fitness data for steps, health metrics and measurements for heart rate, and sleep data without sharing their Google password. Course notebooks retrieve the authorized data into each student's own Colab session; the helper does not retain OAuth credentials or raw health records. If a learner selects Fitbit data and explicitly runs the optional OpenAI summary cell, the notebook sends OpenAI the exact displayed selected-day raw steps, heart-rate, and sleep JSON; Google/OAuth credentials are never included, and the helper itself sends nothing to OpenAI. Students submit only the notebook and are instructed to clear raw-live-JSON outputs and remove any echoed DataPoint name, exact timestamp, or device/data-source detail from the retained summary first; course staff do not intentionally receive raw records when that unenforced instruction is followed. If Fitbit input was used, the retained AI summary and written comparison/reflection remain derived from Google Health data. Course staff will read those retained materials for grading under the student's explicit pre-consent acknowledgment. Live data is optional: the synthetic path supports the same required work and equivalent credit for students who do not consent to this staff access, and students can decline or revoke access at any time without penalty.
